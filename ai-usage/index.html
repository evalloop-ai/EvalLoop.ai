AI USAGE DISCLAIMER

Understand the
limits of AI.

EvalLoop AI uses AI-based systems to assist with evaluation, analysis, comparison, and quality assessment. This page explains important limitations and responsible-use principles.

EFFECTIVE DATE 01 OCT 2026 VERSION 1.0

01 / AI EVALUATION

Evaluation is an analytical aid

EvalLoop AI is designed to help users inspect and measure AI-generated outputs. Depending on the tool, an evaluation may consider factors such as factuality, instruction following, relevance, completeness, safety, fluency, or other quality dimensions.

AI evaluation is not equivalent to absolute truth. An evaluation result represents the output of an evaluation process and should be interpreted within its context.

02 / EVALUATION RESULTS

Scores are not guarantees

Scores, labels, explanations, recommendations, rankings, or other evaluation outputs may be influenced by the submitted content, evaluation criteria, model behavior, available context, and other factors.

EXAMPLE

A response receiving a high evaluation score does not necessarily mean that the response is completely correct, unbiased, safe, or appropriate for every situation.

03 / AI ERRORS

AI systems can make mistakes

AI systems may generate incorrect, incomplete, outdated, inconsistent, or misleading information. An evaluation system can also fail to identify an issue or incorrectly identify an issue.

This means that both the AI response being evaluated and the evaluation itself may contain errors.

04 / HUMAN REVIEW

Human judgment remains important

EvalLoop AI is intended to support human evaluation and analysis, not eliminate appropriate human judgment.

Where accuracy, safety, compliance, financial impact, employment, education, healthcare, legal matters, or other significant consequences are involved, users should apply appropriate human review and independent verification.

05 / SUBMITTED CONTENT

Think before you submit

Users may submit prompts, responses, documents, reference information, or other material to evaluation tools.

Users should avoid submitting confidential information, passwords, authentication credentials, highly sensitive personal information, proprietary information, or other information that they are not authorized to share.

RESPONSIBLE USE

Before submitting content, consider whether the content is appropriate to send to an external AI processing service.

06 / IMPORTANT DECISIONS

Do not rely on AI alone

EvalLoop AI should not be used as the sole basis for decisions that could materially affect a person's rights, safety, finances, employment, education, healthcare, legal position, or access to important services.

Evaluation outputs should be reviewed alongside relevant evidence, context, professional judgment, and applicable requirements.

07 / AI PROCESSING

AI processing may involve third-party technology

EvalLoop AI may use third-party infrastructure, model providers, APIs, hosting services, security services, or other technology providers to operate certain features.

When evaluation requests are processed through such services, submitted information may need to be transmitted to the relevant provider in order to generate the requested result.

Please review the applicable Privacy Policy and the information presented by the relevant feature before submitting sensitive material.

08 / NO GUARANTEE

AI quality can change over time

AI models, evaluation methods, datasets, prompts, infrastructure, and supporting technologies can change. As a result, the same input may produce different results at different times or under different configurations.

EvalLoop AI does not guarantee that an evaluation will identify every issue in an AI-generated response or AI system.
