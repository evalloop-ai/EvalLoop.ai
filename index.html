09 / FAQ

About EvalLoop AI.

EvalLoop AI is a platform focused on AI evaluation, practical evaluation tools, benchmarks, and free knowledge about evaluating AI systems.
The platform is designed to support LLM responses, prompts, RAG systems, AI agents, multimodal systems, safety behavior, and broader AI quality evaluation.
EvalLoop AI is being developed with a strong focus on free and accessible evaluation tools and learning resources.
EVP is the planned evaluation engine family within EvalLoop AI. Different EVP profiles focus on areas such as general evaluation, factuality, reasoning, safety, RAG, multimodal systems, agents, and benchmarking.
EVP profiles are evaluation-engine concepts. Their underlying models and evaluation methods may evolve as EvalLoop AI develops.
