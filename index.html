FAQ

Questions, answered.

EvalLoop AI is an AI evaluation platform designed to help users analyze the quality of AI-generated responses across dimensions such as factuality, relevance, instruction following, completeness, fluency, and safety.

EvalLoop AI is planned with a free-to-use tier that includes usage limits. Additional capabilities may be introduced in future versions.

The initial application focuses on evaluating text-based AI responses. Future versions can expand into multimodal evaluation including vision, speech, and other AI outputs.

The evaluation application can connect to supported language models through its evaluation backend. The model layer can evolve independently from the EvalLoop interface.

A response comparison workflow is planned for the application, allowing users to evaluate multiple outputs against the same prompt or rubric.

The first version is designed as a web application. Desktop downloads can be introduced in a future release.
