EvalLoop AI is an AI evaluation and knowledge platform focused on evaluating LLMs, RAG systems, AI agents, multimodal systems, safety, benchmarks, and AI quality.

The current evaluator supports AI response evaluation. The platform is being expanded toward model comparison, prompt testing, RAG evaluation, agent evaluation, safety testing, multimodal evaluation, and regression testing.

Yes. EvalLoop Knowledge is intended to provide free conceptual and practical education about AI evaluation, including LLMs, RAG, agents, safety, benchmarks, and evaluation methodology.

EVP is the planned specialized evaluation-engine architecture for EvalLoop. Different engines can focus on different evaluation problems such as factuality, reasoning, safety, RAG, multimodal systems, agents, and regression testing.

The long-term architecture is designed to use different models and evaluation strategies for different jobs. The underlying model layer can evolve independently from the EvalLoop evaluation interface.

The current project is being developed with free access as a core goal. Specific usage limits and future capabilities may change as the platform develops.
