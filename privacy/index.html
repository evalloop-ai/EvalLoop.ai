PRIVACY POLICY

Your data. Your privacy.

EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 1, 2026 · EVALLOOP AI

EvalLoop AI respects your privacy and aims to be transparent about how information may be collected, processed, and used when you interact with our website and evaluation services.

01 / INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Information you provide

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide when using EvalLoop AI, contacting us, or interacting with features of the platform.

  • Name and contact information submitted through forms.
  • Email address and other information included in a message sent to us.
  • Information you voluntarily submit while using evaluation features.

Technical information

Website infrastructure, hosting, security, analytics, or other service providers may process technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, IP address, timestamps, and interaction data, depending on the services enabled on the platform.

02 / EVALUATION DATA

Content submitted for evaluation

EvalLoop AI provides tools that may allow users to submit prompts, AI-generated responses, reference information, or other evaluation content.

Important: Do not submit confidential, highly sensitive, proprietary, financial, authentication, or personal information into an evaluation request unless you have determined that doing so is appropriate.

Evaluation requests may be transmitted to backend infrastructure and third-party AI model or API providers used to generate evaluation results. The providers and processing architecture may change as EvalLoop AI develops.

03 / HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Why information may be processed

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating EvalLoop AI services.
  • Processing evaluation requests and generating evaluation results.
  • Responding to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Maintaining platform security and preventing abuse.
  • Understanding and improving the platform and its functionality.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.
04 / DATA SHARING

When information may be shared

EvalLoop AI does not treat your information as something to sell. Information may nevertheless be processed or shared where necessary to operate the service.

  • Service providers that support hosting, infrastructure, security, analytics, communications, or AI processing.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.
  • Parties involved in a business transfer, restructuring, or similar transaction, where applicable.
05 / COOKIES & TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies and similar technologies

EvalLoop AI may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies where needed for website functionality, preferences, security, or other legitimate platform purposes.

If analytics or other optional tracking technologies are enabled, their use may involve third-party providers processing technical or usage information according to their respective policies.

06 / DATA SECURITY

Protecting information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no website, network, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should consider this when deciding what information to submit.

07 / YOUR RIGHTS

Access and control

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other forms of control over your data.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify a request before taking action.

08 / POLICY CHANGES

Keeping this policy updated

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EvalLoop AI develops new features, changes its infrastructure, or responds to legal or operational requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
