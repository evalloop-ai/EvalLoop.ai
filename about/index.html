About EvalLoop AI

Building better AI evaluation.

EvalLoop AI is building practical infrastructure for evaluating AI systems, measuring model quality, and helping teams understand whether their AI actually performs the way it should.

Start Evaluating ↗ Explore Labs
01 / Why

AI systems need measurement.

AI is becoming part of products, workflows, research, and everyday applications. But producing an answer is not the same as producing a good answer.

Evaluation makes quality measurable. It helps teams identify factual errors, instruction failures, hallucinations, safety issues, retrieval problems, multimodal mistakes, and other weaknesses before they become larger problems.

EvalLoop AI is designed to make that process easier, clearer, and more accessible — without requiring every evaluator to become a machine-learning engineer.

01

Practical Evaluation

Evaluation should work on real AI outputs and real product scenarios, not only research benchmarks.

02

Clear Measurement

Quality dimensions should be understandable, measurable, and useful for making decisions.

03

Continuous Improvement

Evaluation is not a one-time check. It is a continuous loop between models, data, measurements, and improvement.

02 / Ecosystem

One evaluation ecosystem.

EvalLoop AI is being developed as an ecosystem rather than a single evaluation screen. The goal is to connect practical evaluation, knowledge, experimentation, and future benchmarking in one place.
