ABOUT EVALLOOP AI

Building better AI evaluation.

EvalLoop AI is an AI evaluation and LLM quality platform focused on making AI evaluation more practical, understandable, and accessible.

AI systems need measurement.

AI models can generate impressive answers, but useful AI systems require more than generation. They need to be evaluated for factuality, relevance, instruction following, completeness, safety, fluency, and other quality dimensions.

EvalLoop AI is being built as a practical environment for testing those dimensions and turning evaluation into a repeatable part of AI development.

PLATFORM FOCUS
AI QUALITY

Evaluation tools, practical labs, knowledge resources, benchmarks, and specialized evaluation engines.

APPROACH

Measure first. Identify weaknesses. Improve systematically.
